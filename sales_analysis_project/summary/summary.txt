Sales Analysis Project Summary

This project analyzes sales data using time series and category-based charts.

1. Line Chart (Sales Over Time):
   Used to identify sales trends across different dates. It helps understand
   growth or decline patterns over time.

2. Monthly and Quarterly Aggregation:
   Monthly and quarterly bar charts summarize sales performance for better
   period-wise comparison and decision-making.

3. Category Comparison:
   Bar chart highlights differences in total sales among categories such as
   Electronics, Clothing, and Grocery.

4. Category Share (Pie Chart):
   Pie chart shows the percentage contribution of each category to total sales,
   making it easy to identify the dominant category.

Conclusion:
Electronics contributed the highest share of sales, followed by Clothing and
Grocery. Sales showed a gradual increasing trend over time.
